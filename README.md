# Hi, I’m Jacksen.

I’m working on ways to make complex ideas easier to hold, develop, and use with language models.

My interest is not in declaring complexity unnecessary. It is in helping people identify what matters, make it addressable, and keep working with it over time—without needing a maze of specialized tools.

I’m developing **PGX (Pointer Geometry Exchange)**, a lightweight natural-language format for giving concepts stable, reusable identities in LLM conversations and documents. PGX sits between ordinary language and graph practice: it lets people name pieces of an idea, connect them, cite them, and reuse them while staying close to the way people actually write and think.

I’m also building **[Parmesan](https://github.com/jacksenpierce/parmesan)**, a local, SQLite-backed tool for durable structured work with LLMs. The goal is simple: open a conversation, bring in your material, and develop a knowledge structure that can survive the chat.

## Current work

- Developing PGX and collecting rigorous, reproducible case studies.
- Building Parmesan as a practical home for structured LLM work.
- Exploring mechanistic interpretability, applied AI, language-model interaction design, and software that gives regular people more agency.

The work is early and experimental. I’m interested in examples, objections, collaborators, and case studies that test what holds up.
